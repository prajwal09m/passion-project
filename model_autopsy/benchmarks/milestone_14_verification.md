# M14 Verification 2.0 (Phase 7)

falsify.falsification_plan records prediction, experiment,
outcome, survival, and uncertainty for every high-confidence
finding. confidence_layers separates observation, measurement,
mechanism, causal, generalization, and reproduction; causal and
generalization stay low without designed interventions and new
projects, and high observation confidence never auto-creates
causal confidence. structured_report separates confirmed, strong,
plausible, unresolved, rejected, untested risks, and
inconclusive. Flagship: confidence observation high, mechanism
low, causal low; verification grade not-reproduced via an
independent tool is reported as uncertainty, not disproof. Tests
in test_m14_systems.py.
