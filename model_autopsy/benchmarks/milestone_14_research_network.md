# M14 Research Network (Phase 8)

network.relationships builds shared-column edges between projects
as transfer hypotheses only. propose_transfer marks every proposal
as hypothesis requiring project-specific evidence. validate_transfer
confirms only with moving evidence on the target and logs
helped/misled. A discovery in one project never becomes fact in
another. Combined with m12 knowledge transfer outcomes and the
portfolio scheduler, long-running queues share one atomic budget.
Tests: edge construction, hypothesis marking, evidence-gated
validation in test_m14_systems.py.
