WHAT THIS BUNDLE IS NOT
-----------------------
It is not a claim of method validation. The simulation battery is a
predeclared falsification exercise on synthetic data; it cannot establish
the week-level exchangeability assumption on the real data, and no
diagnostic can prove that assumption. FWER control on real data is not
guaranteed by these results. The governance-suite test flake (1 failure
in 10 runs, identity not captured) remains open and is disclosed in
packet §8. The sealed holdout has not been accessed. Nothing here adopts
method (b), signs or ratifies any decision, implements or freezes
anything, or authorizes any launch.
