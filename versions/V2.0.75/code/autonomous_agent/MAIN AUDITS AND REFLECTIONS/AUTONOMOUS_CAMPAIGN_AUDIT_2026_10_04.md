## 3. Acceptance-Rate Audit (8 ACCEPTED / 8 attempted)

This section separates **mechanics (what the system actually did)** from **selection-bias
mechanisms (structural reasons acceptance was likely)**. It deliberately does not label the
rate itself as either suspicious or valid.

### 3.1 How hypotheses were generated

- The 8 research questions are a **static, human-authored registry** (`ResearchPlanner.BASE_DIRECTIONS`
  in `core.py`): data_quality (priority 1.00), validation (0.99), feature_engineering (0.92),
  calibration (0.82), error_analysis (0.78), model_complexity (0.72), target_design (0.65),
  model_architecture (0.60). The campaign ran **only hypotheses 3, 4 and 8** — exactly the three
  covered by the recipe library. Hypothesis text is fixed per direction; nothing invents new
  questions at run time.
- Spec *instances* are genuinely generated: each spec composes recipe + hypothesis + a parameter
  tuple + parent version + grounding evidence into a full `ExperimentSpec` with provenance
  (`RecipeSpecProvider._build_spec`). That generation is deterministic, not sampled from a model
  (`llm_involved: false`; no LLM provider exists in this system).

### 3.2 Evidence that existed BEFORE each experiment

Every spec's `provenance.grounding_evidence` records, at generation time:

1. `established_baseline`: `BASELINE_RECONSTRUCTED_2026_10` with pipeline fingerprint
   `d103e4c9…ccf1` (registry: `baseline_registry.json`).
2. `prior_findings_in_direction`: the three most recent prior experiment records in that
   direction — for a00 specs these are all **pre-campaign REJECTED** records; for a01/a02 specs
   they include the campaign's own earlier ACCEPTED runs.

The recipe also pre-declares `required_evidence = (established_baseline,
prior_findings_in_direction)`; if either is missing the proposal is refused
(`MISSING_EVIDENCE`). Evidence availability was therefore a hard precondition, not decoration.

### 3.3 How candidates were prioritized and selected

- **Direction selection** — `ResearchPlanner.plan` scores each eligible hypothesis:
  `priority + 0.18·success_rate + 0.08·exploration + stagnation_bonus − readiness_penalty`,
  tie-broken by `(−attempts, direction)`. The code comment states it explicitly:
  *"Lightweight UCB-style exploration; does not reward raw noisy metric deltas."* The planner
  therefore **cannot see or exploit metric values** when choosing a direction.
- **Capacity filter** — before planning, `Supervisor._autonomous_capacity` calls
  `generation_capacity` and passes `allowed_directions`; directions without recipe coverage or
  budget are excluded. This filter, not the score, determined which of the 8 directions were
  runnable (3 of 8).
- **Parameter selection** — `RecipeSpecProvider._propose` uses
  `params = grid[len(attempts) % len(grid)]`: a **deterministic round-robin over the reviewed
  cartesian grid**, independent of any outcome. The observed sequence matches index 0, 1, 2 in
  each recipe's sorted grid exactly (calibration: bins 5 → bins 10; feature: (LR,500) →
  (LR,1000) → (LightGBM,500); model: (B,500) → (B,1000) → (C,500)). **There is no mechanism by
  which a promising parameter combination could be preferred over a weak one.**
- **Recipe selection** — first recipe in fixed library order whose `direction` matches the
  hypothesis. No scoring, no ranking.

### 3.4 Was rejection possible under the actual decision rules?

Yes, structurally, at multiple layers:

1. **Pre-execution refusals** (7 codes): `DISABLED`, `UNSUPPORTED`, `MALFORMED_RECIPE`,
   `MISSING_EVIDENCE`, `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED`, `DUPLICATE` (two independent layers: attempt ledger
   + adapter `SpecRegistry`), `INVALID_PARENT`.
2. **Execution-time gates**: `ExperimentSpec.validate()` mechanical contract; adapter baseline
   gate; original-tree integrity fingerprint before and after; six mandatory scientific checks.
3. **Decision rules** (`recipe_harness.decision_criteria_pass`): `delta ≥ 0.0`,
   `CI lower ≥ −0.01`, `folds ≥ 3`, plus calibration margins for the calibration recipe.
   A negative point delta, a CI lower below −0.01, or a Brier/ECE degradation beyond the
   margins → REJECTED, candidate destroyed.
4. **Supervisor-side revalidation**: `_validate_outcome` re-checks the ACCEPTED record and
   artifact hashes before `VersionManager.promote`.

**Empirical proof the stack rejects:** the same gate stack rejected **141 of 141**
pre-campaign experiments (17–18 per direction, all 8 directions), recorded in the same
`agent_state.json`. Within this campaign, refusals/blocks/rejections = 0.

### 3.6 Selection-bias mechanisms (reported separately, as required)

These are **structural properties of the design**, each independently sufficient to make an
ACCEPTED-heavy outcome more likely than a fair coin-flip over "research experiments" in general:

1. **Direction-level coverage filter.** Only 3 of 8 registered research directions were
   executable autonomously. The five uncovered directions (data_quality, error_analysis,
   model_complexity, target_design, validation) — which include the harder, more falsifiable
   questions — had **zero chance of being attempted**, despite carrying the highest priorities
   (1.00, 0.99, 0.78, 0.72, 0.65) in the planner.
2. **Recipes re-test comparisons whose ordering is already evidenced.** The feature recipe
   compares *nested* arms (A⊂B⊂C⊂D) where the frozen study already reports arm B > arm A and
   the archive reports a small C-minus-B delta; the model recipe compares LogisticRegression vs
   LightGBM where LightGBM is the established champion estimator; the calibration recipe
   measures non-degradation of arms already in service. These are confirmatory designs: the
   scientifically interesting outcome (a surprising failure) was possible but unlikely by
   construction.
3. **Lenient, predeclared acceptance margins.** `ci_lower_min = −0.01` accepts "absence of
   meaningful harm" rather than requiring evidence of gain. Under the stricter counterfactual
   `ci_lower_min = 0.0`, **3 of 8 experiments would have been REJECTED** — model a00
   (CI lower −0.000916), model a01 (−0.000320), and feature a02 (−0.009906) — because their
   confidence intervals include zero. The acceptance rate is materially sensitive to this
   predeclared margin.
4. **Budget spent on parameterized repeats.** Attempts a01 (three of them) re-ran already-known
   configurations differing only in bootstrap count or bin count — parameters that cannot alter
   the point estimates (see §4). Repeat acceptance was therefore near-certain by construction,
   consuming 3/8 of the campaign's information budget on precision/robustness re-checks rather
   than on novel tests.
5. **No outcome-blindness at the evidence layer, but outcome-blindness at the selection
   layer.** Selection could not see metric values (3.3); however, the *evidence* fed to each
   recipe (prior findings, frozen-study results) is exactly the evidence that establishes the
   expected ordering, so grounding and bias point the same direction.

**What the system did NOT do (no evidence of):** preferential parameter cherry-picking,
suppressing rejections, weakening gates after seeing results, retrying rejected specs, or
running unreviewed designs. Gates and margins were fixed in the recipes before activation and
were not modified during the campaign (`spec_recipes.py` mtime predates activation; hash in
appendix).

## 8. Selection-Bias / Scientific-Validity Assessment

### 8.1 Which description is legitimate?

**The weaker claim is the accurate one:**

> "An autonomous system that **selected from a closed set of reviewed experiment recipes**,
> instantiated them with deterministic parameters under a predeclared budget, executed them in
> isolated workspaces, and promoted outcomes that passed predeclared gates."

The stronger claim — "an autonomous system that **generated and evaluated research
experiments**" — is only defensible if "generated" is read narrowly as *spec instantiation*.
The system genuinely did compose new, complete, self-describing experiment specifications at
run time (parameter tuples not fixed in advance, parent version resolved from live champion
state, evidence grounded in live records, refusal paths live), and it genuinely evaluated them
under gates it did not alter. But:

- the **research questions are static and human-authored** (8 registry entries, unchanged);
- the **experimental designs are static and human-authored** (3 recipes, reviewed pre-campaign);
- the **decision rules are static and human-authored** (margins fixed pre-activation);
- there is **no LLM or learned generator** anywhere in the loop (`llm_involved: false` is a
  structural fact, not a label);
- no new mechanism, feature, model class, or hypothesis was invented during the campaign.

Honest framing: **autonomous execution and spec instantiation over a reviewed recipe library;
not autonomous formulation of research.**

### 8.2 How should the 8/8 streak be presented?

**Primarily as a phenomenon requiring further investigation — with a secondary, well-evidenced
engineering reading.** Specifically:

- It is *sound evidence for engineering reliability*: 8/8 end-to-end completions, 0 crashes,
  0 safety violations, deterministic promotions (§11 A).
- It is *weak evidence for autonomous scientific discovery*: the three recipes are confirmatory
  by construction (§3.6), three of eight attempts were parameterized repeats whose point
  estimates were guaranteed to match earlier runs (§4), and three of eight passed only
  because of the predeclared `ci_lower_min = −0.01` margin — under a zero floor the streak would
  have been 5/8 (§3.6 item 3).
- It is *not evidence of generalization*: no holdout was used, `uses = 0`; all numbers are
  development-OOF on one frozen dataset.
- The appropriate follow-up investigations are listed in §10 (stricter CI gate, holdout-evaluation
  policy, novel-direction recipes, negative controls / deliberately adversarial recipes).

**No claim of fraud, tampering, or gate-weakening is supported by the evidence**, and none is
made here: gates, margins and budgets are byte-stable in source from before activation through
campaign end (hash in appendix), and the pre-campaign 141/141 rejection record demonstrates
the identical stack rejecting freely when designs are weaker.

## 10. Limitations

1. **Only 8 executed experiments** — a single afternoon's run; no repeated campaigns, no
   cross-day stability of the loop itself.
2. **Closed recipe library of 3 recipes** — 5 of 8 registered research directions were
   structurally unreachable; no novel mechanism/feature/hypothesis generation occurred.
3. **Bounded generation capacity** (3+3+2 = 8) — the campaign ended by design, not by scientific
   saturation; "exhaustion" means *budget/coverage exhaustion*, not *question exhaustion*.
4. **Selection effects** — coverage filtering, confirmatory recipe design, and the lenient
   `ci_lower_min = −0.01` margin all push toward acceptance (§3.6); under a zero floor the
   streak would be 5/8.
5. **Repeated deterministic experiments** — 3 of 8 attempts (a01 round) were parameterized
   repeats whose point estimates were fixed by construction; they demonstrate reproducibility
   but add little independent evidence.
6. **Single dataset, single protocol** — one frozen development event table (61,045 rows,
   100,026,639 bytes, SHA `232de7db…4d0`), folds 2023–2025, fixed hyperparameters; no external
   validation.
7. **No prospective evidence** — holdout SEALED with uses = 0; all metrics are development
   OOF; no trading simulation (`backtest: NOT_RUN`; real-money trading prohibited).
8. **Comparative scope** — claims are feature-arm/estimator-level; fixed-universe
   survivorship risk and reconstruction drift (49 known feature differences vs historical
   V1.0.1) are inherited and documented, not resolved.
9. **Operational scaling issue** — `original_fingerprint()` hashes the whole tree including
   `versions/`, so pre-run integrity cost grows with every promotion (24 min by cycle 8);
   observed, not a scientific limitation, but it caps future campaign length.
10. **Single-run provenance quirks** — layered timestamps with skew, dual ID schemes, and no
    per-run environment lockfile hash (§7).

## 11. Final Verdict (separate conclusions)

**A. Engineering reliability — PASS, with high confidence.**
8/8 end-to-end cycles completed autonomously; 24/24 role commands rc=0 with no timeouts; zero
errors/crashes (`last_error: null`, empty stderr); crash-safe append-only ledgers; at-most-once
attempt semantics held; promotions, state updates and the final self-shutdown
(`research_exhausted` → stop → lock release) all executed without human intervention or
cleanup.

**B. Reproducibility — PASS (deterministic core), partial (parameter sensitivity).**
The a01 round reproduced every point estimate to all 6 persisted decimals under changed
bootstrap/binning parameters; shared input artifacts are byte-identical across all 8 runs
(dataset SHA, pipeline fingerprint, study outputs). Confidence intervals legitimately move
with `n_boot`, and ECE moves with bin count — these are parameter-dependent quantities, not
reproducibility failures. Byte-identical *evaluation records* were not achieved (timestamps/
run ids embedded).

**C. Safety / leakage control — PASS.**
Holdout sealed and untouched (uses = 0, directory absent at root and in all 8 workspaces);
leakage/walk-forward/purge/robustness/data-integrity checks PASS ×6 in all 8 runs;
forbidden-surface enforcement plus before/after original-tree fingerprints with no anomaly;
network-denial shim present in every workspace; no model deserialization; historical V1.0.1
evidence, baseline records and ledgers unchanged; no evidence of any violation found.

**D. Autonomous experiment generation — PARTIAL / qualified PASS.**
The system autonomously *instantiated, executed, evaluated, promoted and iterated* complete
self-describing experiment specs under live state (parent resolution, evidence grounding,
duplicate/budget refusals) — that is genuine autonomous spec generation. It did **not**
author research questions, designs, or decision rules; those are a fixed reviewed set, and no
LLM exists in the loop. The accurate claim is the weaker one in §8.1.

**E. Scientific validity — WEAK-to-MODERATE; confirmatory, not discovery.**
The experiments were executed honestly under predeclared gates with proper purge-aware
chronological validation and block-bootstrap uncertainty, so the *measurements* are
trustworthy for what they are. But the campaign mostly re-confirmed comparisons whose ordering
was already evidenced (nested arms, known-superior estimator), spent 3/8 budget on repeats,
and accepted 3 runs on a margin that tolerates CIs crossing zero. No new scientific question
was answered; nothing here establishes out-of-sample or economic value. The 8/8 streak should
be reported with the §3.6 bias mechanisms attached, not as a standalone success metric.

**F. Evidence for model improvement — MODERATE for configuration selection on dev folds;
NONE for prospective or economic improvement.**
The campaign establishes, on the frozen development folds, that (i) market-structure arm B
dominates arm A (+0.0707 under LR), (ii) arm-C LightGBM reaches the best pooled OOF AUC of the
campaign (0.683633) with a strictly positive model-class CI (+0.0233 [0.0080, 0.0363]) and
improved Brier/ECE, and (iii) year-subgroup deltas are positive for all three years in the
final experiment. It does **not** evidence real-money, backtest, or holdout performance —
`backtest: NOT_RUN`, holdout `SEALED, uses = 0`, and the reconstruction-not-V1.0.1 disclaimer
remains mandatory.
