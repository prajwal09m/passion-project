Subject: Independent statistical review request — permutation-based
FWER-controlled testing procedure (with simulation code)

Body:

I am seeking an independent expert review of a preregistered statistical
decision procedure before it is adopted in an automated research harness.
Attached is a self-contained review packet with the simulation evidence
report, the simulation code, summary results, a hash manifest, and
reproducibility instructions. No data-confidentiality obligations apply
beyond not redistributing the materials; the code is reproducible on your
side (see the MANIFEST in the attachment).

The procedure under review: week-level arm-label sign-flip p-values
(one-sided, M = 1,000 flips, +1 correction) combined with Holm step-down at
alpha = 0.05 across predeclared families, used as the sole formal
family-wise test in a holdout-free automated experiment harness. A
simulation battery (SIM-1 through SIM-5) was run under predeclared
protocols; it falsified two alternative candidates but cannot establish
real-data validity. The central open question is the week-level
exchangeability assumption on real data.

Scope — please answer these six questions in a written assessment:
1. Does the proposed sign-flip test's null (sharp within-week arm-label
   exchangeability) match the intended scientific claim, given that the
   claim of interest concerns the aggregate AUC difference rather than the
   sharp null?
2. Do shared-training dependence (both arms' OOF predictions from models
   fitted on overlapping training windows) and week-to-week serial
   dependence materially undermine marginal calibration? Would you
   recommend block-level flips (per the bootstrap's 4-week block unit) or a
   different construction?
3. Do the simulations (SIM-1...SIM-5, proxy frames of 800-3,600 rows
   versus the real ~61k-row frame) adequately test the proposed use case?
   What cell, frame size, or mechanism is missing in a way that blocks
   adoption?
4. Is the documented CI-vs-p disagreement reconciliation statistically
   defensible: formal decisions via Holm-adjusted p-values only, with the
   percentile-bootstrap CI kept as a disclosed provisional/effect-size
   functional (the two disagree at 1.4-10.5% near the null boundary)?
5. Are the Holm family definitions and the missing-member rule
   (an unavailable member test counts toward family size m and is assigned
   p = 1.0, rendered as "unavailable", never formally REFUTED) coherent
   and conservative as claimed?
6. What additional evidence, if any, is necessary before adoption?

Deliverable: a written assessment answering the six questions, with your
reasoning, limitations, and qualifications. Please state any conflict of
interest and the scope boundaries of your review.

This is a methods-and-code audit request, not an analysis job. The packet
distinguishes observed evidence, synthetic evidence, assumptions, and
unresolved questions; please do not treat the simulation results as proof
of real-data validity — that limitation is the reason for this review.
