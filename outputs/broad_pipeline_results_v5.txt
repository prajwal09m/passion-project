Interpretation:
  The v5 backtest shows positive returns (55.60%) with strong risk-adjusted metrics
  (Sharpe 1.93, Profit Factor 1.40), a significant improvement over v4's negative
  performance. This suggests the event-based portfolio accounting and proper
