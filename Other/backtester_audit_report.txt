3. CAPITAL ALLOCATION REVIEW
============================================================

CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION (ml_demand_zone_trainer.py line 648):
  position_size = min(cash, equity * risk_per_trade / stop_loss_pct)
  where: equity = cash + sum(p['size'] for p in open_positions)

ISSUE IDENTIFIED:
- Uses total equity (cash + unrealized gains) for position sizing
- This allows using unrealized profits to fund new positions
- Unrealized gains are not available cash for new entries
- This is unrealistic for actual trading

CORRECT IMPLEMENTATION:
  position_size = min(cash, cash * risk_per_trade / stop_loss_pct)
- Should use only available cash, not total equity
- Unrealized gains should not fund new positions

IMPACT ASSESSMENT:
- Current implementation may overestimate available capital
- Could lead to taking more/larger positions than realistic
- May explain part of the performance improvement from v4 to v5
